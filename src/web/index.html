🌌 Claugravity

          v0.1 (Beta)
        

        
      

      

        

          Claugravity is a free, open-source local bridge that lets Google Antigravity install, run, and update plugins, skills, guidelines, and MCP servers created for the Claude ecosystem.
        


        

          ⚠️ Important Notice:
          

            Antigravity indexes skills, rules, and plugins during its startup. Whenever you install, update, or remove a skill or plugin in Claugravity, restart your Antigravity app or IDE so it re-reads your configuration.
